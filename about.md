I am currently a PhD Candidate at CSE. Dept., HKUST, Hong Kong and an Assistant Lecturer at CS. Dept., Assiut University, Egypt

### More Information

I am currently a 4th year PhD student of Computer Science at Hong Kong University of Science and Technology. My research interests covers a range of topics from cloud/data center networks and systems, wireless networks, congestion control and traffic engineering. My current projects are focused on these areas: design and implementation of communication protocols well-suited for data center networks as well as exploring their congestion and traffic engineering aspects. I am currently adviced by Prof. Brahim Bensou and we are part of the Networking and Systems Lab.

Previously to enrolling in the PhD program, i was (and still) an Assistant Lecturer at the Faculty of Computers and Information, Assiut University, Egypt. I hold a B. Sc and M. Phil Degrees of Computer Science earned from Assiut University, Egypt.

My Career Goal: to succeed in an environment of growth and excellence and earn a job which provides me job satisfaction and self development and help me achieve personal as well as organizational goals. In addition, to create value and recognition on work place by producing the best result for the research community. Finally, to continuously strive for higher achievement in life and establish myself as a perfect and accept challenging work and contribute forward towards the success of the organization i am affiliated with by hard work and applying acquired skills.
